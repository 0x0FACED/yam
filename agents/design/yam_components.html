9. Bottom Sheets YamBottomSheet

Canonical bottom-sheet container and composition for fast food confirmation.

Canonical Architecture

Bottom sheets are the primary confirmation surfaces in YAM. They MUST be composed strictly of existing library components without custom ad-hoc styling.

  • 1. Handle: 36×4px pill centered, r99
  • 2. Header: YamMealChip + Product Title + Close Icon
  • 3. Quantity: YamQuantityControl (one unit select)
  • 4. Presets: YamQuickAmountPresets (5 items)
  • 5. Macros: YamNutritionSummary (flat row)
  • 6. CTA: YamPrimaryButton (solid #a78bfa)
